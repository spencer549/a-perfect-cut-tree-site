Living in Lakeview means dealing with tight lots, massive old-growth trees, and the constant threat of a Gulf storm. You can’t just hire “a guy with a saw” when a 60-foot oak is leaning over your roof or a utility line.

At A Perfect Cut, we provide arborist-led tree care designed for the specific needs of Lakeview and Jefferson Parish. We know the local soil, we understand the parish codes, and we have the gear to handle removals in even the most cramped backyards.

What We Do for Your Property

Safe Tree Removal

In Lakeview, trees often grow right up against property lines. We specialize in controlled removals, taking trees down piece-by-piece to protect your home, your neighbor’s fence, and your landscaping.

  • Hazardous Trees: If it’s dead, rotting, or leaning, we’ll get it down safely.
  • Tight Spaces: We use specialized equipment to work around power lines and structures.

Emergency Storm Cleanup

When a storm hits, you don’t have time to wait. We answer calls 24/7 and offer emergency response to clear fallen trees from driveways, roofs, and power lines.

Pruning & Health Checks

Proper trimming isn’t just about looks, it’s about storm prep. By thinning the canopy and removing dead weight, we help your trees “breathe” during high winds, making them much less likely to snap when a hurricane or thunderstorm rolls through.

Stump Grinding

A leftover stump is just an invitation for termites. We grind stumps deep below the surface so you can replant, pave, or just enjoy a flat, clean lawn again.

Lakeview Tree Service FAQs

Do I need a permit to cut down a tree in Lakeview?

In many cases, yes. Jefferson Parish has specific ordinances (especially for “Protective” or “Quality” trees like Live Oaks and Cypresses). If your tree is over a certain diameter, you’ll need a permit before the saw touches the bark. We’re familiar with the local permit office and can help you navigate the paperwork to avoid heavy fines.

Can you get your equipment into my small backyard?

Lakeview lots are notorious for narrow alleys and “sliver” side-yards. We don’t just roll a heavy truck onto your grass. We use compact, specialized lifts and climbing gear designed to get into tight spaces without destroying your flower beds or cracking your driveway.

How do you handle trees tangled in power lines?

With all the overhead lines in the 70123 area, this is common. You should never touch these yourself. We coordinate with the local utility companies to ensure the power is handled safely before we begin pruning or removal.

Is stump grinding necessary, or can I just leave it?

In South Louisiana, leaving a stump is basically an open invitation for Formosan termites to move in near your home. We strongly recommend grinding the stump at least 6 to 12 inches below grade to eliminate the habitat and let you regrow grass or a new garden bed.

When is the best time of year to prune my trees for hurricane season?

Ideally, you want to “thin out” your canopy between late winter and early spring. This reduces the “sail effect” before June 1st. If your trees are heavy and overgrown, thinning them allows high winds to pass through the branches rather than catching them and uprooting the whole tree.

Do you handle the debris, or will it sit on the curb?

We don’t leave your yard looking like a disaster zone. Our team hauls away all logs, branches, and woodchips the same day the job is done. If you want to keep some wood for a backyard fire pit, just let us know, and we’ll stack it neatly for you.
